鉛筆射擊 支援

手指按住紙面往要射的方向推，放手就從筆尖射出一條線；線碰到圈就射下。射下每關規定的數量就過關。線碰到尺或紙邊會反彈，射進墨漬要扣一次中繼。

Pencil Shot Support

Touch the paper and push toward where you want to shoot, then let go to shoot a line from the pencil tip. When the line touches a circle, it's hit. Hit the number of circles each level asks for to clear it. Rulers and paper edges bounce the line; an ink blot costs a relay.

FAQ
