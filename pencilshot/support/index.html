鉛筆射擊 支援

手指在紙上慢慢移是轉方向，放手不會射；往前快速推出去再放手，才從筆尖射出一條線，推越快線越長。線碰到圈就射下，紙上的圈全部射下才過關。線碰到尺、橡皮擦或紙邊會反彈，經過膠帶會變慢，碰到圖釘會停下，射進墨漬要扣一次中繼。道具：+1 多一次中繼、箭頭加速、橡皮擦章擦掉墨漬。共 500 關。

101 關起「飛越書桌」，每 100 關換一站（筆記本、方格紙、點點紙、牛皮紙、便條紙），還有文具障礙：板擦會把線吸住停下、黑板很滑線跑更遠、原子筆會滾來滾去、量角器的圓弧反彈角度會變、橡皮筋彈回來更快。

常見問題

進度會不見嗎？進度存在裝置上；刪除 App 會一併刪除進度。

可以關掉聲音或震動嗎？首頁按「設定」，可以切換音效、震動。

怎麼從頭玩？「設定」→「重設進度」會清掉全部星星，從第 1 關開始。

廣告的隱私選項在哪？「設定」→「追蹤權限」可以開關 iOS 追蹤；歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者在「設定」→「廣告隱私選項」可以隨時改 Google 的同意選擇。

廣告怎麼處理資料？請見隱私權政策。

Pencil Shot Support

Slide your finger slowly to aim; letting go then does not shoot. Push forward quickly and let go to shoot a line from the pencil tip; the faster the push, the longer the line. Hit every circle on the paper to clear the level. Rulers, erasers and paper edges bounce the line, tape slows it, a pin stops it, and an ink blot costs a relay. Items: +1 gives an extra relay, arrows speed the line up, the eraser badge wipes out all ink. 500 levels.

From level 101 you fly across the desk, with a new paper every 100 levels (notebook, graph paper, dot paper, kraft paper, sticky note) and stationery obstacles: a chalk eraser stops the line dead, a chalkboard is slippery so the line goes farther, pens roll back and forth, a protractor's curve changes the bounce angle, and a rubber band bounces the line back faster.

FAQ

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Can I turn off sound or vibration? Tap "Settings" on the home screen.

How do I start over? Settings > Reset Progress clears all stars and starts from Level 1.

Where are the ad privacy options? Settings > Tracking Permission opens the iOS tracking switch. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can change their Google consent any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices.

How do ads use data? See the Privacy Policy.
