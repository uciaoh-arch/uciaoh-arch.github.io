鉛筆射擊 支援

手指在紙上慢慢移是轉方向，放手不會射；往前快速推出去再放手，才從筆尖射出一條線，推越快線越長。線碰到圈就射下，紙上的圈全部射下才過關。線碰到尺、橡皮擦或紙邊會反彈，經過膠帶會變慢，碰到圖釘會停下，射進墨漬要扣一次中繼。道具：+1 多一次中繼、箭頭加速、橡皮擦章擦掉墨漬。共 100 關。

Pencil Shot Support

Slide your finger slowly to aim; letting go then does not shoot. Push forward quickly and let go to shoot a line from the pencil tip; the faster the push, the longer the line. Hit every circle on the paper to clear the level. Rulers, erasers and paper edges bounce the line, tape slows it, a pin stops it, and an ink blot costs a relay. Items: +1 gives an extra relay, arrows speed the line up, the eraser badge wipes out all ink. 100 levels.
