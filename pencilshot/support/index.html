鉛筆射擊 支援

手指在紙上慢慢移是轉方向，放手不會射；往前快速推出去再放手，才從筆尖射出一條線，推越快線越長。線碰到圈就射下，紙上的圈全部射下才過關。線碰到尺、橡皮擦或紙邊會反彈，經過膠帶會變慢，碰到圖釘會停下，射進墨漬要扣一次中繼。道具有 200 種，線碰到就拿到，看圓框顏色分七類：綠色是中繼、星星與達成條件的獎勵；紫色改變這一發（磁鐵、穿透、加速、彈力球、轉彎、重力、穿邊、鬼影、連鎖等）；藍色整關有效；粉紅色清掉墨漬、文具或一塊地方；紅色直接射下圈（飛鏢、雷射、光束、炸彈、手電筒、骰子等）；青色移動筆；橘色移動圈。右下角的小字是啟動時機：停下時、反彈時、射下圈時或整關；沒有小字就是馬上。每種第一次出現時，紙下方會寫它做什麼。整十關是挑戰關，每 50、100、1000 關更難。共 3000 關。

101 關起「飛越書桌」，每 100 關換一站，共 30 站（筆記本、方格紙、點點紙、牛皮紙、便條紙、作業簿、稿紙、樂譜……），還有文具障礙：板擦會把線吸住停下、黑板很滑線跑更遠、原子筆會滾來滾去、量角器的圓弧反彈角度會變、橡皮筋彈回來更快。501 關起還有迴紋針（碰到會從同顏色的另一個出來）和削鉛筆機（穿過去會加速），一路飛到第 30 站「書桌盡頭」。

常見問題

進度會不見嗎？進度存在裝置上；刪除 App 會一併刪除進度。

可以關掉聲音或震動嗎？首頁按「設定」，可以切換音效、震動。

怎麼從頭玩？「設定」→「重設進度」會清掉全部星星，從第 1 關開始。

廣告的隱私選項在哪？「設定」→「追蹤權限」可以開關 iOS 追蹤；歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者在「設定」→「廣告隱私選項」可以隨時改 Google 的同意選擇。

廣告怎麼處理資料？請見隱私權政策。

Pencil Shot Support

Slide your finger slowly to aim; letting go then does not shoot. Push forward quickly and let go to shoot a line from the pencil tip; the faster the push, the longer the line. Hit every circle on the paper to clear the level. Rulers, erasers and paper edges bounce the line, tape slows it, a pin stops it, and an ink blot costs a relay. There are 200 items; touch one with the line to get it. Ring color shows the type: green for relays, stars and prizes you earn; purple changes this shot (Magnet, Pierce, Boost, curves, Gravity, Wrap Around, Ghost, Chain and more); blue works for the whole level; pink clears ink, stationery or an area; red hits circles directly (darts, lasers, beams, Bomb, Flashlight, Dice); teal moves the pencil; orange moves circles. The small letter at the corner says when it works: on stop, on bounce, on hit or all level; no letter means right away. The first time each appears, the bottom of the paper says what it does. Every 10th level is a challenge, and every 50th, 100th and 1000th is harder. 3000 levels.

From level 101 you fly across the desk, with a new paper every 100 levels, 30 stops in all (notebook, graph paper, dot paper, kraft paper, sticky note, workbook, manuscript paper, sheet music…) and stationery obstacles: a chalk eraser stops the line dead, a chalkboard is slippery so the line goes farther, pens roll back and forth, a protractor's curve changes the bounce angle, and a rubber band bounces the line back faster. From level 501 there are paper clips (the line pops out of the same-colored twin) and sharpeners (they speed the line up), all the way to stop 30, Desk's End.

FAQ

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Can I turn off sound or vibration? Tap "Settings" on the home screen.

How do I start over? Settings > Reset Progress clears all stars and starts from Level 1.

Where are the ad privacy options? Settings > Tracking Permission opens the iOS tracking switch. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can change their Google consent any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices.

How do ads use data? See the Privacy Policy.
