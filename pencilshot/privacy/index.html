隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-26。適用 App：Pencil Shot（鉛筆射擊）。開發者：Yu Chiao Huang。

我們蒐集什麼

鉛筆射擊不需要註冊，我們不蒐集、不儲存您的姓名、聯絡方式或任何個人資料。關卡進度、星數與音效開關只存在您的裝置上，不會傳給我們。

廣告

為了免費提供遊戲，App 上方會顯示 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告。AdMob 可能蒐集裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼（IDFA）、大致位置、廣告互動，以及當機、效能與其他診斷資料，用於投放與衡量廣告、分析與防止詐騙。若您在 iOS 的「追蹤」提示選擇不允許，AdMob 不會取得 IDFA，只會顯示非個人化廣告，遊戲完全照常。您可隨時於「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「追蹤」變更。歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者會先看到 Google 的同意表單。Google 如何處理資料請見 Google 隱私權政策。

兒童

本遊戲不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

政策若有更新，會公布在本頁並更新生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-26. App: Pencil Shot. Developer: Yu Chiao Huang.

What we collect

Pencil Shot requires no account. We do not collect or store your name, contact details or any personal data. Level progress, stars and the sound setting stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising

To keep the game free, a Google AdMob banner is shown at the top of the screen. AdMob may collect device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate location, ad interactions, and crash, performance and other diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. If you decline the iOS tracking prompt, AdMob does not receive the IDFA and shows non-personalized ads; the game works the same. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form first. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google uses data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
